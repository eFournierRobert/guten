Behind the notebook

A place to
pay attention.

Not everything needs to be a big idea. Some things just need a place to land.

Fieldnotes is a journal about the things that make an ordinary day feel a little fuller: a different route home, an afternoon making something useful, a quiet hour with a notebook.

The rule is simple: notice something, spend a little time with it, write it down. There is no publishing schedule and no race to keep up.

A small site, by design

This is a fictional journal made to show what you can build with Guten. The articles start as Markdown, the pages share a little HTML, and everything you see is a static file. Feel free to use it as a starting point for your own corner of the web.
