My notebook is not impressive. The cover is bent, the first few pages are shopping lists, and there is a coffee ring across a drawing I can no longer explain.

That is why I like it. It doesn't ask me to organise anything before I begin.

## What goes in

A sentence overheard on the bus. Measurements for a shelf. The name of a tree to look up later. Sometimes a whole page about a problem; sometimes just three words that will mean nothing next week.

I date the pages and leave the rest alone. No colour system, no index, no obligation to write every day.

## What comes back out

Once in a while I turn back through it. Most of the notes have already done their job. A few connect to something I am thinking about now. Those get a small mark in the margin.

Paper makes that kind of accidental rediscovery easy. You look for a measurement and find a sentence instead.

If you want to try it, start with whichever notebook is already in a drawer. Put it in a pocket. Write the first ordinary thing you notice. That's the whole setup.
